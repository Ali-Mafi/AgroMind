## Approved long-term capability direction — 2026-10-30

The owner has confirmed that **all** seven workstreams in [the Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) are intended for implementation, with details, budgets, success metrics and dates to be agreed phase by phase. This product decision is not proof that the features already work, nor permission to release code.

1. **Phase 0 — Security & Stability:** F01/F02 PR #52 release gate, operational reliability and production/device acceptance.
2. **Phase 1 — Weather Intelligence 2.0:** source-aware, validated, localized agricultural weather and warnings.
3. **Phase 2 — Smart Irrigation 2.0:** explainable soil/crop/weather-based water planning.
4. **Phase 3 — AgroMind AI Assistant:** privacy-safe, farm-aware and evaluated conversational decision support.
5. **Phase 4 — Premium & Billing:** entitlements, reliable Zibal payments and transparent subscriptions.
6. **Phase 5 — Satellite & Farm Intelligence:** field boundaries, dated imagery, NDVI and vegetation trends.
7. **Phase 6 — AgroMind IoT:** secure sensor monitoring followed by separately approved, hardware-safe automation.

Cross-cutting nonfunctional requirements: tenant isolation, secure changes, agricultural operational safety, data provenance, explainability, FA/EN/RTL and other supported locale/unit preferences, responsive PWA, accessibility, privacy and cost controls. Phases 1–6 are **planned**, not shipped. Existing foundational features must be extended rather than rewritten.

The earlier **Future** concepts (drones, ownership verification, marketplace) remain discovery ideas outside this seven-phase commitment, pending a distinct prioritization decision.
