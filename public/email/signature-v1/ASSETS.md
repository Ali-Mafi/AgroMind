# Signature Premium email assets

- `wheat-field.jpg`: real photograph by **Shubham Patil**, published 2025-03-04, CC0, from https://wordpress.org/photos/photo/85367c5c45/ . Source: https://pd.w.org/2025/03/85367c5c4517afd54.48314224-1536x864.jpeg . Cropped to 1200 × 420 and JPEG-compressed for 600 × 210 email presentation. Representative landscape, not a customer's farm.
- `logo.png`: PNG email-compatible derivative of the established `public/logo/agromind-mark-132-v1.webp` brand mark.
- The shield, highlights, Instagram outline and Telegram paper-plane PNGs are small original vector-path renders. Decorative icons have empty alt text; their adjacent labels provide meaning. Social identities and links are text, usable with images blocked.
- `footer-curve.png`: transparent green arc, 1200 × 72, rendered at 600 × 36. If blocked, the dark-green footer becomes rectangular and remains readable.

All image requests are first-party HTTPS under `https://agromind.ir/email/signature-v1/`. No external font, tracking pixel, script, remote image service or base64 payload is sent in an email. Versioned paths avoid changing visuals in already delivered messages.
